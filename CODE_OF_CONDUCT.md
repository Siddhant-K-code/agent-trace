# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Assume good intent while giving
clear technical feedback. Respect privacy, identities, experience levels, and
different viewpoints.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, personal
attacks, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate these standards. They may also temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns to
[siddhantkhare2694@gmail.com](mailto:siddhantkhare2694@gmail.com). Reports will
be reviewed privately and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.
